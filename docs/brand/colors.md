# KodRoot colors

Burgundy for the sign, near-black for the name, warm paper. The sign is stamped. The name is written under it.

## Essential

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Burgundy | `#7C2430` | 124, 36, 48 | Symbol and bar, on light and dark grounds, favicon tile |
| Ink | `#1A1614` | 26, 22, 20 | Wordmark, text, monochrome |
| Paper | `#F6F1E8` | 246, 241, 232 | Light ground, wordmark on dark |
| Night | `#1A1214` | 26, 18, 20 | Dark ground |

## Supporting

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stone | `#6E675E` | 110, 103, 94 | Secondary text on Paper |

## Contrast

| Pair | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink on Paper | 16.0:1 | Text, wordmark |
| Paper on Night | above 15:1 | Text, wordmark on dark |
| Burgundy on Paper | 8.7:1 | Symbol, bar |
| Burgundy on Night | 1.9:1 | Symbol and bar on dark. The name stays Paper, which is the readable part. |

Do not use Burgundy for body text.
