# KodRoot typography

## Wordmark

The name in the logo is a custom drawing, not a font. It sits under the bar of the radical. The stroke is light, about 10.2% of the cap height, and the arms rise 1.05 for every 1 they run. Spacing is even. Do not reset that drawing in another typeface and call it the logo.

## Supporting text

For documents and interfaces, not for the logo: Source Sans 3, regular and semibold, in `fonts/`. SIL Open Font License. The license text is `fonts/OFL-source-sans.txt`. Fallback: `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

Supporting letter-spacing stays at 0. Short uppercase labels may use about 0.08em.
